# Garrett's voice

Learned from Garrett's line-by-line copy review of badgairbros.com and acrefile.com
(28 September 2026, 37 lines judged). Update this file whenever he corrects wording again.

## What he keeps

- **Specific and real.** Real field names, acres, dates and counts from the record:
  "620 photos in 38 minutes of flying, one battery swap." "10 minutes for these 65.3 acres."
- **Farm words and farm scenes.** "The county plat book on the kitchen table." "Call before you
  haul." "The brown patches worth walking, the corner that always comes up thin."
- **Personal, first-hand reasons.** "We built Acrefile because every report we got came in a
  different app, on someone else's account, and none of it added up to a record of the ground."
- **People with a voice.** "The one who tells us when a map is wrong."
- **Short plain statements that are true.** "The grower owns every file." "A person signs every
  recommendation." "Text us a field."

## What he cuts or rewrites

- **Stock sentence shapes.** He cut or replaced every one of these:
  - "X, never Y" ("Accuracy is recorded, never assumed." Cut.)
  - "X, not Y" ("Then you read the field, not a pixel." Cut. "Signed, not summarized." Replaced.)
  - "No X, no Y" closers ("No cloud, no monthly fee." "No login, no app to install.")
  - Three-beat fragments ("Ask. We fly. It lands on your record.")
  - Two-fragment slogans outside the motto ("One record. Yours.")
- **Cute or slogan labels.** "Same ground, two eyes" and "A file for every acre" were cut. Headings
  and labels say what the section is or does.
- **Brochure words.** "For good", "empower", "seamless", "insights", "solution".
- **Our jargon in place of farm words.** "Grower-owned record", "orthomosaic" (say "map"),
  "revoke" (say "turn off"), "six numbers in words" (say "soil test in plain words").
- **Stating the obvious.** "Waterway · not in the acres" (every farmer knows).
- **Anything not quite true.** "No login" (growers sign in by emailed link; only share links need
  none). "What every farm app shows you" (some use paid imagery). Check each claim against the
  product and the record before writing it.

## Rules

1. One idea per sentence. Short sentences.
2. Name the job in farm words: what gets flown, filed, signed, kept.
3. Use a real number from the record instead of an adjective.
4. Buttons say what happens next, in sentence case: "Ask about a flight", "Get started".
5. No emoji, no exclamation marks, no hype.
6. The motto is fixed: **Your ground. Your data. Your call.** Don't imitate its rhythm elsewhere.
7. Leave out what the reader already knows.
8. If a line could appear on any ag-tech site, rewrite it until it could only be ours.
9. Badgair Bros writes British spellings (colour, centimetres); Acrefile's app writes American.
   Keep each consistent with itself.

## Examples from the review (before → after)

- "Drone field mapping and a grower-owned record for the farms of the Chippewa Valley. We fly it,
  your agronomist signs it, and the file is yours for good." → "Drone maps of your fields, filed
  in a record you own. We fly it, your agronomist signs it, you keep it."
- "Satellite sees a field. We see the rows." → "The satellite shows a field. Our flight shows the
  rows."
- "The photos are matched, meshed and blended into a single orthomosaic you can measure from.
  … No cloud, no monthly fee." → "The photos are matched and blended into one map you can
  measure from … on our own computer. Nothing to subscribe to."
- "Share links you can revoke" → "Share links you can turn off"
